\section*{AI Collaboration Disclosure}

This work was developed in sustained collaboration with AI coding assistants. The authors maintain a continuous session log (\texttt{agent\_log.md}) recording decisions, deviations, and AI-generated artifacts throughout the project; this disclosure section is drafted continuously from that log rather than assembled retrospectively, following the precedent set by Matthew Schwartz in ``Vibe Physics.'' A final version enumerating the specific roles of each assistant, the scope of human review, and the provenance of code, figures, and text will appear in the camera-ready submission.
